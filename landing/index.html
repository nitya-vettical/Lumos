Understanding Color Vision Deficiency

Over 300 million people worldwide live with color vision deficiencies. Here is how Lumos helps.

Protanopia

Red-Cone Deficiency

Affects approximately 1.3% of males. Individuals struggle to perceive red light, causing reds, oranges, and greens to merge into similar muted tones.

Deuteranopia

Green-Cone Deficiency

The most common form (~5% of males). Green photoreceptors are absent or impaired, making red/green distinctions difficult.

Tritanopia

Blue-Cone Deficiency

A rare condition (~0.008% of population) where blue photoreceptors are lacking, leading to confusion between blue/green and yellow/pink.

Achromatopsia

Complete Monochromacy

Total absence of color vision, perceiving the world in shades of gray, black, and white. High contrast rendering becomes vital.
